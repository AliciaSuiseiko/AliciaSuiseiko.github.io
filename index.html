Research approach

How I work on robotics problems

I usually begin with a working physical stack, trace failures across sensing, estimation, planning, and execution, and then narrow the research question to the interface that actually breaks. I am especially interested in learned semantic reasoning, but I treat geometry, timing, safety, and explicit fallback as part of the method rather than deployment details left for later.
